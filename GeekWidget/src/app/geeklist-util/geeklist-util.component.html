This utility is designed to help you find useful stuff in trade geeklists. Enter the geeklist number or URL and Extended Stats will go find that geeklist and point out entries on it that are relevant to you. There are two hyperlinks in each line - the game name links to the BGG entry for the game, the remainder links to the entry on the geeklist.
