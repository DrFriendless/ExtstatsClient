Would you like to increase your H-index? This chart gives you some insight into how it's calculated and what you can do to increase it.

Your H-index is currently {{hindex()}}. The graph below denotes your {{width()}} most-played games, with most-played on the left and decreasing to the right. The number of plays is the height of the orange/red bar (but some of those bars might actually go off the top of the page). The red part is the number of plays in the last year. Your H-hindex is defined as the width of the biggest square that can be drawn inside the orange bars.

To make that square bigger (i.e. increase your H-index) you need to fill in more spaces to the top and right with orange. That can be done by playing less-played games again, thus making their orange bar taller and bringing them to the left. Whenr the square immediately to the top-right of the black box turns orange, the black box can expand because your H-index has increased.
